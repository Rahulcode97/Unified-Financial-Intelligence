Case study · Designed & built independently

Three payment gateways, one console.

Every month, the finance team pulled records from CCAvenue, PayPal and the bank, then spent days matching them in spreadsheets. I built the system that does it for them. It fetches, matches and reconciles all three, flags anything that doesn't add up, and produces the report in one click.

The problem

A week of finance work, buried in spreadsheets.

Before
  • 40+ hours a cycle downloading and cross-referencing three systems by hand
  • Tax and GST errors surfaced up to two months later
  • Every report took a day-long audit
  • No single view of revenue, fees or refunds
After
  • One-click monthly report, generated in minutes
  • Automatic matching of gateway references against bank statements
  • GST and tax mismatches flagged when they happen
  • Fee and revenue-driver analysis that reduced the need for extra admin headcount

Architecture

Each layer uses the right tool for the job.

LayerTechnology & why
Frontend / desktopReact for the web; Tauri for a lightweight, secure native shell where a desktop app is needed.
Backend & APIsNode.js and PHP for gateway APIs; Python for reconciliation logic, refunds and analysis.
Database / cloudMySQL plus NoSQL on Google Cloud for fast reads across large transaction sets.
SecurityGranular role-based access, a role × page permission matrix, and encrypted transaction logs.
