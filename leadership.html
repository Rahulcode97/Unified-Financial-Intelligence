The technical bar I set

What I evaluate — and what I won't compromise on

Every candidate goes through the same lens: can they ship in our stack, reason about data, and grow into AI-native workflows?

Dimension What I look for
Full-stack fluency Comfort across frontend, API layer, and data — not siloed. Can they trace a feature from UI to database and back?
Data & analytics SQL literacy, pipeline thinking, and the instinct to validate numbers before presenting them to finance or leadership.
AI-native mindset Uses LLM tools productively (not as a crutch), understands token cost, and can integrate APIs like OpenAI, Gemini, or Vertex safely.
Ownership & judgment Takes ambiguous problems, proposes a approach, ships incrementally, and communicates trade-offs — especially on infra cost and security.
Team fit Clear communicator, receptive to feedback, and willing to maintain systems others will depend on — not just greenfield heroics.

How I onboard new hires

Week 1 — Context & codebase

Architecture walkthrough, access setup, and a guided tour of the finance platform — reconciliation flows, RBAC, and where data lives.

  • Read production patterns, not toy examples
  • Shadow live debugging and deployment
  • First small PR with paired review

Weeks 2–4 — Ship with guardrails

Own a scoped feature or integration slice — Telegram bot endpoints, report exports, API hardening — with me as technical reviewer until they're unblocked independently.

  • PR standards, testing expectations, cost awareness
  • Intro to our AI tooling workflow (Cursor, Claude Code)
  • Direct exposure to stakeholder feedback loops
